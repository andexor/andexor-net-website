Surface container for grouping content — service cards, stat tiles, form panels.

```jsx
<Card hover as="a" href="/seo">
  <Badge tone="accent">SEO</Badge>
  <h3>Technical SEO audits</h3>
  <p>Find and fix what's holding your rankings back.</p>
</Card>
<Card ink>Dark blue variant for emphasis blocks.</Card>
```

`pad` (on by default), `hover` for clickable lift, `ink` for the dark blue variant.
