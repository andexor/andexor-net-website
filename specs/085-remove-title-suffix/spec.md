# Feature Specification: Remove the Title Suffix

**Feature Branch**: `98-cleanup-titles`

**Created**: 2026-10-28

**Status**: Draft

**Input**: User description: "Remove the " | Andexor Network" postfix from the title on all pages."

Changes specs 028, 029, and 030, which added the " | Andexor Network" suffix to every page title.

## What the site does today

- Every page title is the page's headline text followed by " | Andexor Network": the home page, each Markdown content page, and the Page not found page.
- The meta description is the headline text alone, with no suffix.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Titles without the suffix (Priority: P1)

A visitor opens any page and sees a browser tab title that is just the page's headline text, such as "Web Development", with nothing after it.

**Why this priority**: This is the whole change.

**Independent Test**: Open the home page, each content page, and a made-up address that shows the Page not found page, and read each tab title.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** any content page, **When** it loads, **Then** its title is its headline text and does not end with " | Andexor Network".
2. **Given** the home page, **When** it loads, **Then** its title is its headline text and does not end with " | Andexor Network".
3. **Given** an address that does not exist, **When** the Page not found page loads, **Then** its title is "Page not found" with no suffix.
4. **Given** a content page whose Markdown sets its own title, **When** it loads, **Then** the title is exactly that text.

### Edge Cases

- A new Markdown page added later gets a suffix-free title with no extra work.
- The company name still appears in the page itself (header, footer, and copy). Only the title changes.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: No page title MUST end with " | Andexor Network", on the home page, every content page, and the Page not found page.
- **FR-002**: The title MUST otherwise be what it is today: the page's title set in its Markdown, else its first headline, else its file name; the home page's headline; and "Page not found".
- **FR-003**: The meta description, the search-engine indexing rules, and everything else on the page MUST NOT change.
- **FR-004**: The documentation that describes the suffix (the content authoring guide and the comments that mention it) MUST be corrected.
- **FR-005**: The site's rules still apply: no link underline at rest, on hover, or on focus, straight quotes only, always dark, and no copy, wording, color, or spacing tests.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: On 100% of pages, the title equals the page's headline text and contains no " | Andexor Network".
- **SC-002**: A page added by writing one new Markdown file has a suffix-free title without any other change.

## Assumptions

- The title is the page's headline text, as it is today, so the Page not found title is "Page not found".
- The existing check that the title matches the headline stays, without the suffix, because it covers behavior (title follows the headline) and not wording.
- Built pages are regenerated by the normal build; nothing is edited in the built output by hand.
