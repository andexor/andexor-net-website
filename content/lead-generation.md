# Lead Generation

More qualified customers. More sales.

---

## Every business needs more customers.

Let's start by thinking about the
kind of audience you want to attract
and how to identify them.

- Who are they?
- What are they like?
- What do they do?
- Where do they operate?
- What are their needs?
- What products or services do you offer that they might find useful?
- What other deciding factors separate you from the competition?

We can either give you a raw list of contacts
that you call and qualify,
or we can qualify them before handing them to you.
Just let us know.

We have a few tools and methods available to
find more leads for you. We may use several of them
and bring you a report with data blended from multiple sources.

---

## Need to adjust the parameters?

The definition of an ideal target audience
is not always known clearly upfront.
It can be a journey of discovery sometimes.
Let us know anytime you want to make some adjustments.
We'll make them right away so you can see the effects
in your next report.

||

## Need to adjust the frequency?

Let us know when you have more than you can handle.
We can pause the feed for a while to give you
time to catch up. Just let us know when you
are ready for more again and we will resume the search.
