# Growth Marketing

We want to tell everyone about you. And reach them where they are.

## Do you need branded email?

Yes you do. If you're using a third-party email system,
you're not using your brand. You're missing an easy
opportunity to spread your brand name with every email you send.
Let's get you setup with email coming from your own domain name
to add a professional touch to it.

Even though you get plenty of sales just the way it is,
there will be some customers who won't take it seriously
if your email address does not match your website.
Let's take care of that gap so you can eliminate
that mental blocker and go for those customers too.

We will also get you setup with the right configuration
settings so that your emails are much less likely to be
dropped or automatically flagged as spam.

We can even get your emails migrated
from your current system to your new, branded
email on the same domain as your website.

## Do you send out a newsletter?

If you send a daily, weekly, monthly, or occasional newsletter
to your subscribers, then you know how easy it is to use it
for free marketing. You can always use it to make product
and service announcements, educate buyers, and answer questions.
If you have not ventured into this yet, let's get you started.

We'll even set you up with the right configuration so that
if your mailing list grows to more than 5000 recipients,
you won't be considered as a spammer.

## Do you have a blog?

If not, today is a great day to start one.

If you've got something to say on a regular basis,
this is the place to do it first.
Then you can always break it down and remix it
in social media posts that link back to the blog.
Host the blog on your website and it will
help increase your domain authority.

Many business owners think they don't
have anything to say.
Maybe because no one has asked them yet.
We can get you started by asking questions.
Your answers often lead to more questions.
We can write it all down for you,
put your name on it, and publish it for you.
And we can keep doing this anytime a new
question comes up.

## Need help with social media campaigns?

Let's take a look at which social media platforms
you are currently on and review others to see
which ones are the right platforms for your company,
your products, your services, and your industry.
If there is another platform that might serve you better,
let's get you setup there and start using it.

Let's examine some of your previous campaigns
and see how well they performed for you.
Are there sales that can be attributed to
specific posts or ads as part of a campaign?

Do you like to write, but just don't want
to bother with posting to multiple locations?
We can do that for you.

Or would you rather outsource the whole process,
including researching and writing articles,
blog entries, newsletters, social posts, and ads?
We can do that too. We will still need to pick
your brain so we can customize the writing
and make sure it adds value to the reader.

Talk to a Marketing Specialist today to get started.

## Want to issue press releases?

You may think that press releases are just for
big companies making big announcements.
Traditionally, yes, but today anyone can issue
a press release.
If you are a small and growing company,
you should issue one for every little thing you do.
If you are an established company
and desire to expand your borders, this is for you too.
