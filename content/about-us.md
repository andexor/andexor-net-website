# About Us

Who we are. What we have done. Where we are going.

---

## Who we are

Andexor Network, Inc. was founded in 2010
in Atlanta by [Ed Jenkins](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ejenkins/).
The original vision at the time was simply for web hosting alone.
That and a desire to introduce customers to each other
so they can meet each others needs for multiplied growth.

---

## What we have done

The reality was a stretch of time doing consulting work,
followed by a long dormant period.
During this time, Ed worked on other projects,
gaining more technical and leadership experience
in exciting new areas.

||

## Where we are going

Today, Andexor is ready to help you
solve many more types of issues, including
development, hosting, SEO, lead generation,
and marketing.
And looking forward to introducing you
to other business leaders too!
