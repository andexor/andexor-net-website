## Need insight into what your app is doing?

With our expertise in observability,
we can monitor your system's
logs, metrics, and traces regularly
so you don't have to.
Poor application health can contribute
to a loss in sales just as much as
a healthy system facilitates sales.
Let's put a scope on your systems
to identify what's happening,
where, and why.

In our experience, most production support issues
are the result of a lack of data validation
combined with a lack of proper error handling.
Many systems suffer from the assumption
that nothing will go wrong
and when something unexpected does happen,
a tribal knowledge is all you need.

## Need relief from alert fatigue?

There is nothing quite like having
so many alerts setup that nothing
is important anymore. We know how to
cut through the weeds to reduce or eliminate
excess alerts so you can focus your time on
what really matters.

## Slow application performance driving customers away?

Let our expertise in site reliability engineering

We test applications for data throughput and latency,
then recommend, implement, and monitor changes
in code, configuration, or deployment as needed.

## Analytics

It is important to always keep an eye on

We can surface information about your

may not have been able to see before
and prove theories that were unprovable before.
We can also engineer reports to distinguish
between real events and false alarms,
anomalies, scheduled tasks, and bot traffic.

to make sure the issue does not happen again.
